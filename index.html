Technical art

Where the art job stops being a drawing job.

Portrait to landscape conversion. The title existed in portrait only. I was the only artist on the conversion, working with one developer to rebuild the UI, reel layout and symbol arrangement for a different aspect ratio.

Every decision had to be checked against what the engine could actually do, so most of the real work was deciding where the design had to bend and where it couldn't.

Alongside that: naming and structuring assets so the same layout could be handed to someone else, and keeping the documentation current so it stayed usable after I moved on.

Drawing for a screen you can't predict. A phone, a tablet and a desktop browser all crop the same feature screen differently. So the art is built oversized around a safe area: nothing that matters ever leaves the centre, and the background keeps going in all four directions.

Composition gets harder, not easier. The focal point has to read when the frame is tall and narrow, and still read when it's short and wide, without redrawing anything.

Getting this right at the art stage is what stops a title needing a separate layout pass per device later.
