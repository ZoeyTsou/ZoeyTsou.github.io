The production line

From 2022 I ran this alone. No assistant, no outsourcing, three operator platforms, each with its own delivery spec.

01Feature researchRead the maths, learn what the game actually does
02QA capture setupDefine what needs to be recorded and how
03Gameplay recordingCapture every feature and win state
04Asset replacementFull art pass across the game project
05Promo video editScript, cut, sound, motion graphics
06Marketing packDelivered to three platform specs
One person, all six stages. Research through to final delivery, on every title.
  • I turned marketing pack delivery into a documented system. One spec sheet per platform, reused on every title, so the format questions stopped being questions.
  • I brought AI tools into live production from 2021. Nobody asked me to. The manual steps were the bottleneck, so I went and fixed them.
  • I wrote and maintained the internal documentation the whole process runs on, which is why it survived handover.

Technical art

Where the art job stops being a drawing job.

Portrait to landscape conversion. The title existed in portrait only. I was the only artist on the conversion, working with one developer to rebuild the UI, reel layout and symbol arrangement for a different aspect ratio.

Every decision had to be checked against what the engine could actually do, so most of the real work was deciding where the design had to bend and where it couldn't.

Alongside that: naming and structuring assets so the same layout could be handed to someone else, and keeping the documentation current so it stayed usable after I moved on.
